About Solara

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Solara Property Group is a growing real estate company specializing in residential properties in Calapan City and surrounding areas in Oriental Mindoro, Philippines. We saw a need in the market for a property group that prioritizes the customers experience overe mere transactions.

We focus on making property buying, selling, and renting feel clear, trustworthy, and less intimidating for individuals, young families, professionals, and small investors.

Our Mission

"To provide clear property information, straightforward communication, and professional guidance to help our clients make confident real estate decisions."

The Solara Difference

Unlike generic property advertisement sites, we operate as a professional real estate brand you can trust when making major financial decisions. We prioritize:

  • High-quality presentation properties.
  • Straightforward communication without hidden agendas.
  • Unmatched local market knowledge.
  • Helpful guidance throughout the entire process.
